QUESTIONS

Why isn't it in the App Store?

Gather installs straight from this page, so every church gets updates right away without waiting for app store reviews. It works the same as a store app once it's on your Home Screen.

Is it safe?

Yes. Messages, photos and files are encrypted on your phone before they're sent. Only the people in each chat can read them. Each church's group is private and separate, and a leader approves everyone who joins.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, and it doesn't show ads.

How do I delete it?

Press and hold the icon on your Home Screen, then choose Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android).
